FlowTask Privacy Policy

Version: 2026-06-22

1. Overview

FlowTask is built to help people manage tasks, events, notes, schedules, and focus time. This Privacy Policy explains what information FlowTask handles, why it is used, and the choices available to you.

2. Information You Provide

FlowTask may store information you choose to add, including:

  • Account details such as your email address and sign-in provider.
  • Tasks, subtasks, projects, tags, due dates, planned work times, priorities, estimates, and notes.
  • Events, imported calendar entries, event notes, locations, recurrence details, and follow-up task links.
  • General notes, workspace layouts, saved UI preferences, notification preferences, and backup/restore metadata.
  • Feedback, support messages, tier requests, account deletion requests, and optional diagnostics you choose to send.

3. How FlowTask Uses Information

FlowTask uses your information to:

  • Provide your signed-in workspace across devices.
  • Save, sync, organise, search, display, and restore your tasks, events, notes, and preferences.
  • Run optional features such as AI Assist, Google Calendar sync, notifications, imports, exports, and private sync.
  • Protect the service from abuse, enforce fair-use limits, investigate bugs, and respond to support requests.
  • Improve reliability, performance, and user experience during beta and future releases.

4. Local Device Storage

FlowTask uses browser storage and service-worker caching so the app can load quickly, remember preferences, keep queued changes, and protect your workspace when your device is offline. If you clear browser data before sync finishes, unsynced local changes may be lost.

5. Account Sync And Service Providers

FlowTask uses trusted infrastructure providers to operate the service. Supabase is used for account sign-in and account workspace storage. Firebase Hosting may be used to deliver the public website. Cloudflare Workers may be used for AI Assist, security checks, and optional Google Calendar connector features.

6. AI Assist

When you use AI Assist, the text you submit and relevant workspace context may be sent to FlowTask's AI connector so it can create, update, schedule, or complete tasks and events. FlowTask uses fair-use limits to keep AI Assist available and prevent misuse.

7. Google Calendar Sync

If you connect Google Calendar, FlowTask may read, create, update, and delete calendar events according to the permissions you approve. FlowTask uses this access to sync FlowTask tasks and events with Google Calendar and to import calendar events back into FlowTask. You can disconnect Google Calendar through Google account permissions or FlowTask settings where available.

8. Private Sync And Encryption

FlowTask may offer optional passphrase-based private sync. When enabled and unlocked, task, event, and note content can be encrypted before it is saved to cloud storage. Some account, billing-free plan, sync, diagnostic, and service metadata may remain unencrypted so FlowTask can operate.

9. Sharing And Disclosure

FlowTask does not sell your personal workspace data. Information may be processed by service providers, disclosed when required by law, used to protect FlowTask and users, or reviewed when you send feedback, support requests, tier requests, or account deletion requests.

10. Security

FlowTask uses practical security measures such as account-based access, HTTPS hosting, browser security headers, row-level access controls, optional private sync, and limited public configuration. No online service can guarantee perfect security, so keep your account credentials safe and report suspicious activity.

11. Your Choices

  • You can export a backup from FlowTask settings.
  • You can delete workspace data from FlowTask settings.
  • You can request account deletion from FlowTask settings.
  • You can disconnect optional integrations such as Google Calendar.
  • You can choose whether to send optional diagnostics with feedback.

12. Children And School Use

FlowTask is a general planning service and may be used for school planning where permitted by the user's school, parent, guardian, or account administrator. Users should not add highly sensitive information that they do not need for planning.

13. Changes

FlowTask may update this Privacy Policy as the service changes. The version date at the top of this page shows when this policy was last updated. Continued use of FlowTask after changes means you accept the updated policy.

14. Related Terms

This Privacy Policy works together with the FlowTask Terms, Proprietary License, and Fair Use Policy.

15. Contact

For privacy, account, or support questions, use Help & Feedback inside FlowTask settings, or contact FlowTask through the support channel provided with the service.
